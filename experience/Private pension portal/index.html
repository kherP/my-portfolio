Private pension portal

role: Lead frontend developer

  • React
  • Webpack
  • Sass
  • Jest
  • DotNet
  • Azure DevOps
  • Openshift

overview

Users can view and manage their pension payouts in this portal. They can update their payout duration, payment accounts or even postpone their upcoming payouts.

challenges

  • Complicated business rules. The state of the form fields are toggled based on the business rules. The team came out with a structured data model where Frontend will set the state of the fields based on the status returned.

result

  • Easy maintenance. The business side can easily update the field condition without needing frontend to make any changes.
